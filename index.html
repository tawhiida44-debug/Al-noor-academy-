About Our School

We believe children can do well in class and grow in faith at the same time. Learners follow full Kenyan CBC plus daily Islamic lessons, taught by qualified, caring teachers.

Two pathways. One school day.

Every pupil studies both streams — no choosing between academic success and Islamic upbringing.
